About Me!

I study art history and computer science at NYU, and I'm interested in how we look at, describe, and preserve images. I graduate in May 2027 and I'm looking for an internship or full-time role.

I'm a senior at NYU double majoring in Art History and Computer Science. While some may think the combination to be unconventional, I am drawn to the intersection of art, technology and information. I'm particularly interested in how digital tools and computational approaches can make art and curatorial collections more accessible, organized, and engaging.

Through my studies and my work, I have explored this intersection through museum research and publishing, collections and donor data management, and programming projects focused on problem-solving and information gathering.

MOTIVE, MEANS, AND OPPORTUNITY: Comparing Exhibition Design at The Met and The Brooklyn Museum

Ink & Image, 2025

In 2025, both The Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Brooklyn Museum presented exhibitions showcasing their African art collections. This paper analyzes and compares their exhibition design, spatial layouts, and approaches to the politics of interpretation.
